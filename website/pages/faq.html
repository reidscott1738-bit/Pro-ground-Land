Drainage & grading

Why does water keep sitting in my yard?
Heavy clay holds water on the surface instead of soaking in. Adding topsoil rarely fixes it — the ground usually needs regrading or a catch-basin system.
French drain or grading — which do I need?
It depends on how the water behaves. Water sitting in one spot points to a catch basin; water sheeting across a yard points to grading. The drainage diagnostic gives a starting read.
