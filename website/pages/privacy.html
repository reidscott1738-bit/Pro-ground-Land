Privacy Policy

ProGround Land Management respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect when you request an estimate or contact us, and how we use it.

What we collect

Contact details you provide — name, phone, email, property address — plus any photos or project details you share so we can prepare an accurate estimate.

How we use it

Only to respond to your inquiry, prepare estimates, schedule and perform work, and follow up. We do not sell your information.

Text messages

If you opt in, we may text the number you provide about your estimate or scheduled service. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.

Data retention

We keep inquiry and customer records only as long as needed to serve you and meet legal or accounting requirements.
