Drainage FAQs

How much does yard drainage cost?
Every drainage project is scoped to the yard. The number is driven by linear feet of pipe, how many catch basins, and access for equipment — confirmed with a free site visit.
Do I need a French drain or grading?
It depends on how the water behaves. Water sitting in one spot points to a catch basin; water sheeting across a yard usually points to grading. The diagnostic above gives a starting read.
Will you damage my lawn?
We work carefully and restore disturbed turf. Most lawns recover within a few weeks.
What happens to the water once it is collected?
It is carried in pipe to a suitable outlet — the street, a swale, or an existing drain — away from the foundation.
