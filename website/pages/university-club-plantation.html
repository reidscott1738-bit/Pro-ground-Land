Landscaping that lives up to the neighborhood

University Club Plantation is a master-planned, gated community off Nicholson Drive south of LSU, built around a private championship golf course and set between the Mississippi River and Bayou Manchac. Its villages of established, upper-end homes are exactly the kind of properties where landscaping is expected to look its best year-round, and where a reliable, professional crew matters.

We keep University Club lawns and beds sharp on a set schedule, then handle the bigger work when it comes up — resetting overgrown foundation beds, refreshing mulch and edging, and fixing drainage on the low, water-adjacent lots common near the bayou. One crew, one point of contact, and the kind of communication and follow-through these homes deserve.

University Club Plantation FAQs

Do you service University Club Plantation?
Yes. University Club Plantation is part of our regular Baton Rouge route, for both recurring lawn maintenance and larger landscaping, drainage and renovation projects.
Do you offer recurring lawn maintenance in University Club?
Yes. We keep lawns and beds sharp on a set schedule with mowing, edging, blowing and bed care, billed automatically each month so there is nothing to chase. Residential agreements start around $145 per month, scoped to the property.
Can you get through the gate for service?
Yes. We coordinate gated and monitored access ahead of time so scheduled service happens smoothly, and for recurring maintenance you do not need to be home as long as we have access.
Can you renovate established beds and fix drainage here?
Yes. Many University Club homes have mature landscaping that has outgrown its beds, and the lots near the bayou can sit low. We reset bed lines and plant material, and handle grading and drainage on water-adjacent lots, all from one crew.
How much does landscaping cost in University Club Plantation?
Every property is different. Recurring maintenance, renovations, drainage and installation are each scoped to the property, with a free written estimate and no trip fee.
