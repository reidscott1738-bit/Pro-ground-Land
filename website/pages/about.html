ProGround Land Management was founded in October 2025, after the owner, Andrew Lee, spent over five years learning and understanding the trade and figuring out how it can be improved.

Meet the owner

A local business, run by the person who answers the phone

ProGround Land Management was started with a simple goal: build the kind of landscaping and property maintenance company I would want working at my own home. I am a local business owner based in Denham Springs, and I am still personally involved in the day-to-day operation of the company — from meeting customers and looking at projects to estimating, planning jobs and making sure the finished work meets our standards.

Whether we are maintaining someone’s lawn, fixing a drainage problem or completely renovating their landscaping, I want customers to feel comfortable knowing who they are working with and confident that their property is being taken care of. ProGround is growing, but I never want us to lose the personal side of the business.

With more than five years in lawn, landscape and property maintenance and over 100 properties served across the Greater Baton Rouge and Denham Springs area, the goal has never been to chase job volume — it is to build long-term relationships with the homeowners, families and business owners we work for.

— Andrew Lee, Owner-Operator

Why choose ProGround

Hiring a landscaping company is mostly a leap of faith. A lot of homeowners hesitate simply because they do not know who to choose — will the crew actually show up, will the price on the estimate be the price they pay, and will anyone answer when there is a question?

ProGround was built to take those worries off the table. You get clear communication from the first call, a written scope and price before any work starts, a scheduled date we keep, and the owner involved from start to finish — so choosing us is not a gamble.
