The service

A professional display, none of the hassle

Hanging Christmas lights means ladders, tangled strands, burned-out bulbs and a January afternoon spent taking it all down again. We handle the whole thing for you, so the only part you deal with is flipping the switch and enjoying it.

Questions

Christmas light installation FAQs

What areas of my home can you decorate?
Rooflines, trees and shrubs, columns, walkways, or a combination. Tell us the look you want and we will design a display around it.
Do you take the lights down after the holidays?
Yes. Takedown and removal after the season is part of the service, and we can store your materials off-season so they are ready to go next year.
Do you provide the lights, or do I?
We install professional-grade lights and materials built to hold up outdoors. If you already have lights you want used, we can work with those too.
Do you need exterior power?
Yes. We plan the display around accessible exterior outlets near the areas being decorated, and we will confirm what is available when we quote.
How much does Christmas light installation cost?
It starts at $300 and is scoped to the size of the display, the areas decorated, materials and access. You get a clear quote up front, for free.
When should I book?
Earlier is better. Holiday installation spots fill quickly once the season starts, so reach out and we will get you on the schedule.
